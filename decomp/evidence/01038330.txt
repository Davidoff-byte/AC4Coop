## What this establishes and what it does not

Established by the disassembly: the hull value is a **float returned by virtual slot 0x1B4** on the
object returned by `sub_1038330(entity, 0)`; `0x1B8` is its sibling (max).

NOT established: the class of that object, and therefore whether the value is a raw field read. The
probe must resolve the runtime vtable identity before the value is used by the mod.
